Top-Down Strategy (1D → 1H → 5M)

Practical checklist and visuals derived from your trading notes.

Step 1: Daily (1D) - Set Bias
  • Mark Fair Value Gaps (3-candle imbalance)
  • Mark swing highs and lows
  • Determine Premium (sell) vs Discount (buy) within range
Rule of Thumb: Premium → look for Shorts • Discount → look for Longs
Visual Example - Daily Chart
Step 2: 1H - Confirm Direction
  • Find consolidation (2+ times)
  • Look for Break of Structure in bias direction
  • Confirm an FVG formed during the break
If unclear: Don't trade.
Visual Example - 1H Chart
Step 3: 5M - Execute (9:30–11:00 AM EST)
  • Wait for liquidity sweep (H/L) or tap into FVG
  • Then displacement creating an FVG in your direction
  • Enter on Inverted FVG or strong impulse away from level
Visual Example - 5M Entry
Managing the Trade — Price Weighting
  1. At first target (prev H/L), move SL to 1 candle behind the swing taken.
  2. Trail stop behind subsequent swings to capture runners.
Outcome: You’ll eventually be stopped out — but capture the big legs.
Bonus Confluences
  • SMT Divergence (correlated asset fails to make new H/L)
  • Price ran beyond 7:30 AM or Midnight Open
  • Asian/London H/L taken prior to setup

Risk more with 2+ confluences. Risk less if aggressive or lacking confirmations.

The Fail Flip Model
  1. If higher TF level fails, look for opposite-direction displacement.
  2. Flip bias and apply the same 5M rules.
Key Rules
  • Trade only 9:30–11:00 AM EST
  • Stop for the day after losses equal to your average R:R
  • Journal every trade
  • If unclear — do nothing
  • Hold winners longer than you think
Real Trade Example: EUR/USD Long Setup
1 Daily Analysis (1D Chart)

Price is trading at 1.0780 in the DISCOUNT ZONE (below 50% EQ of 1.0800)

✅ Bias: Look for LONG opportunities

2 1H Confirmation

After consolidation, price breaks structure to the upside with a BOS candle and creates an FVG at 1.0775-1.0780

✅ Confirmation: BOS + FVG formed → Ready to execute on 5M

3 5M Execution (9:45 AM EST)

Price sweeps the previous low at 1.0772, then shows displacement creating a small FVG

✅ Entry: 1.0775 | SL: 1.0770 (5 pips) | TP: 1.0795 (20 pips) | R:R: 1:4

4 Trade Management
  • Price hits first target at 1.0790 → Move SL to breakeven (1.0775)
  • Trail stop behind each 5M swing low as price continues up
  • Final exit at 1.0805 when stopped out → +30 pips profit (1:6 R:R)
🎯 Result: Winning trade following the complete Top-Down methodology. The key was patience waiting for all confirmations: Daily bias → 1H BOS+FVG → 5M liquidity sweep + displacement → Entry in FVG.
