Complete ICT Trading Tutorial

Introduction

This tutorial covers the complete ICT (Inner Circle Trader) top-down trading approach adapted for 1D → 1H → 5M timeframes.

Goal: Learn to identify high-probability setups by aligning multiple timeframes and understanding institutional order flow.

Trading Basics

What You Need to Know First

  • Timeframes: We use three timeframes - Daily (1D) for bias, 1-Hour (1H) for confirmation, and 5-Minute (5M) for execution.
  • Market Sessions: Focus on New York session (9:30 AM - 11:00 AM EST) for best liquidity.
  • Instruments: Works on Indices (NQ, ES, YM, RTY), Forex majors (EURUSD, GBPUSD, etc.), and Metals (XAUUSD).
  • Pips: We measure in pips where 1 pip = 0.1 USD for position sizing.
Why This Approach Works

Institutions move markets in predictable patterns. By understanding where they accumulate orders (liquidity), we can trade with them instead of against them.

Core Concepts

The market always moves between three key elements:

1. Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

Price imbalances that act as magnets for future price action.

2. Highs

Resistance levels where sell orders accumulate (liquidity pools).

3. Lows

Support levels where buy orders accumulate (liquidity pools).

Fair Value Gaps (FVG) Explained

What is an FVG?

A Fair Value Gap is created when price moves so quickly that it leaves an imbalance between candle wicks. This happens in a 3-candle formation.

How to Identify an FVG
  1. Look at 3 consecutive candles
  2. Check if there's a gap between candle 1's wick and candle 3's wick
  3. The gap (candle 2) is your FVG
  4. Mark the top and bottom of this gap
Key Point: Price tends to return to fill these gaps before continuing in the original direction.

Types of FVGs

  • Bullish FVG: Gap below current price (support zone)
  • Bearish FVG: Gap above current price (resistance zone)
  • Inverted FVG: When price closes through an FVG in your direction (entry signal)

Understanding Market Structure

Premium vs Discount Zones

Every price range has two halves:

Premium Zone (Upper 50%)

When price is in the upper half of a range:

  • Look for SHORT opportunities
  • Price is "expensive"
  • Institutions likely to sell
Discount Zone (Lower 50%)

When price is in the lower half of a range:

  • Look for LONG opportunities
  • Price is "cheap"
  • Institutions likely to buy

Break of Structure (BOS)

A Break of Structure occurs when price makes a new higher high (bullish) or lower low (bearish), confirming the trend direction.

Step 1: Daily Chart (1D) - Set Your Bias

What to Do on the Daily Chart

  1. Identify the Range: Mark recent swing highs and lows
  2. Draw 50% Line: Split the range in half
  3. Mark FVGs: Identify any daily Fair Value Gaps
  4. Determine Bias:
    • Above 50% = Premium → Look to SELL
    • Below 50% = Discount → Look to BUY
Important: This bias guides ALL your trading decisions for the day. Don't fight it!

Step 2: 1-Hour Chart (1H) - Confirm Direction

The Market Maker Model

On the 1H chart, look for this specific pattern:

  1. Consolidation: Price ranges sideways 2+ times
  2. Break of Structure: Price breaks out in your bias direction
    • For LONG bias: Makes a higher high
    • For SHORT bias: Makes a lower low
  3. FVG Creation: During the break, an FVG forms
Confirmation Complete! When you see all 3 elements, your bias is confirmed. Move to 5M chart.
If Unclear: If you don't see a clear Market Maker Model, DO NOT TRADE. Wait for clarity.

Step 3: 5-Minute Chart (5M) - Execute the Trade

Trading Window: Only trade between 9:30 AM - 11:00 AM EST (though you can journal trades at any time).

What to Wait For

Setup Requirements
  1. Liquidity Sweep: Price sweeps a recent high or low (grabs stop losses)
  2. OR Tap into FVG: Price touches the 1H Fair Value Gap
  3. Displacement: Strong move in your direction creating a small FVG
  4. Entry Signal:
    • Inverted FVG (price closes through the small FVG)
    • OR strong impulse candle away from the level

Entry Models

Confirmation Entry

When everything aligns perfectly:

  • Daily bias clear
  • 1H confirmation present
  • 5M setup triggers

Risk: Standard position size

Fail Flip Entry

When your bias fails:

  • Expected direction doesn't play out
  • Price shows displacement opposite way
  • Flip your bias and enter with new direction

Risk: Can be standard if clear

Trade Management - Price Weighting

This is THE KEY to profitability. Don't take small profits!

The Price Weighting Method

  1. Initial Target: Set TP at previous swing high/low
  2. When TP Hit: DON'T close the trade
    • Move stop loss to 1 candle behind the swing that was just taken
    • Let the trade run to next target
  3. Repeat: Each time a new swing is taken, trail stop behind it
  4. Exit: You'll eventually get stopped out, but you'll catch the big moves
Result: Instead of 2R winners, you'll catch 5R, 10R, or even 20R moves!

Bonus Confluences

When you have 2+ of these, increase your position size:

SMT Divergence

One correlated index makes a new high/low but the other doesn't. Shows weakness.

Session Opens

Price traveled beyond 7:30 AM or Midnight open price levels.

Session Highs/Lows

Asian or London session high/low was taken before your setup.

Risk Less When: Setup is there but no bonus confirmations, or you're being aggressive.

The Fail Flip Model

What if your bias is wrong? Turn it into profit!

How It Works

  1. Recognize Failure: Market isn't respecting your bias
  2. Look for Displacement: Strong move in OPPOSITE direction
  3. Flip Your Bias: Now look for entries in the new direction
  4. Apply Same Rules: Use 5M entry rules for the flipped direction
Mindset: Being wrong is okay. Not adapting is the problem!

Key Rules - MUST FOLLOW

✅ Trade Only 9:30-11:00 AM EST

Best liquidity and clearest setups happen during this window.

✅ Stop After Max Losses

If your average R:R is 3, stop after 3 losses in a day.

✅ Journal Every Trade

Use this app to track every trade. Review weekly to improve.

✅ If Unclear = Don't Trade

Clarity is king. Forced trades lose money.

✅ Hold Winners Longer

This is the #1 key to profitability. Use price weighting!

Practical Examples

Example 1: Perfect LONG Setup
  1. Daily: EURUSD in discount zone (below 50%), bullish FVG present
  2. 1H: Price consolidates 3 times, breaks structure upward, creates FVG during break
  3. 5M (10:00 AM): Price sweeps Asian low, displaces upward creating small FVG
  4. Entry: Enter on inverted FVG at 1.0850 pips
  5. SL: Below sweep at 1.0845 pips (5 pip risk)
  6. TP: Previous high at 1.0870 pips (20 pip reward = 4R)
  7. Management: When 1.0870 hit, trail stop to 1.0865, let it run to 1.0890 (8R total)
Example 2: Fail Flip SHORT
  1. Daily: XAUUSD in premium, expecting shorts
  2. 1H: Tries to break down but fails, creates higher high instead
  3. Recognition: Bias is failing - flip to LONG
  4. 5M (9:45 AM): Strong displacement upward, creates FVG
  5. Entry: Enter LONG at 1975.5 pips (opposite of original bias)
  6. Result: Turned a wrong bias into a winning trade
